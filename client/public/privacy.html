Hesabu Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Hesabu website at hesabu-sigma.vercel.app and the Hesabu app on Android and Windows.

Hesabu helps chama treasurers turn M-PESA contribution records into a clean, shareable contribution ledger. This policy explains what information Hesabu collects, why, and the choices you have. Hesabu is built and operated by an independent developer in Nairobi, Kenya, and is offered free of charge.

What we collect

Account details
Your name, email address, phone number and a hashed password.
Group and member records
The chama groups you create, member names and phone numbers you add, and each member’s contributions and arrears.
M-PESA records
M-PESA confirmation messages or statement text you paste or upload, and the M-PESA transaction codes, amounts, dates and sender names parsed from them.
Technical data
Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) kept for security and debugging, and the settings you choose in the app (such as light or dark mode), which are stored on your own device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS inbox, files or photos unless you explicitly pick something to upload or paste.

How we use it

Only to provide the service described above: to run your account, show you your own data, let you share what you choose to share, keep the service secure, and fix problems. You are responsible for having your members’ permission to record their contributions. Members’ data is visible only to people you add to that group.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure (currently Vercel, Render and a managed PostgreSQL database) that may be located outside Kenya. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your profile and records inside the app.
  • Deletion — open Account → Delete my account in the app to erase your login and every chama you created immediately (your password is required; chamas run by other treasurers keep their books, unlinked from you). If you can no longer sign in, email gnm7208@gmail.com from the address or phone number on your account; deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply. Shared links you created stop working once your account is deleted.
  • Location and camera — permissions can be revoked at any time in your browser or device settings; the app keeps working without them.

Kenyan users have the rights set out in the Data Protection Act, 2019, including the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children

Hesabu is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above will change and the app will point you to the new version.
